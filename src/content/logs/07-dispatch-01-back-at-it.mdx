Hey internet,

It's time for my first behind the scenes update since I've started relaunching my creative efforts.

For those of you who don't know me (and let's face it, that's most of you), I'm Cody and I publish under the name S.C. Barrus.

I was once pretty deep into the self-publishing game, but after a series of life events, I fell out of it for a very long time.

I turned 40 last year, and I took a beat to think back on the things that really make me happy. One of those things was connecting with all of you through publishing my writings, going out into the world and doing events, and shooting the shit with other creatives.

So I'm back at it.

My first realization: I completely left my audience behind and now they are gone. If you're reading this, maybe you're one of them rediscovering me. I hope I didn't let you down too hard in my absence. I've done a lot of growing up, and I'm going to treat you all much better this time around. I won't take you for granted.

On the surface, now seems like maybe the worst time in history to build a creative platform. People are scared that AI will snatch away all the creative opportunities. I'm going to do it anyway. I hope I can give you worlds that make it worth your while.

The world I'm focused on now is that of [KULT](/). It's the best thing I've ever written, and I hope you enjoy it as I tease out the world on this website drop by drop until the book is done. I'll tell you now, the book is dark as fuck. It's not dark for the sake of being dark. There are some really sweet moments in it, and some really characterful ones too.

I cried while writing some chapters (sometimes for a character, and once because I'd actually pulled off a difficult 5,000-word stretch, and it worked). I cringed at writing others. But I love it, I really do, and I can't wait for you to read it. Hopefully you will laugh and cry and cringe and occasionally say, "What the fuck?" That would make me happy.

I'll share more later. But before I go, I want to lament one thing. Let's all pour one out for the death of the em dash — this little piece of punctuation you see right here — because it has always been one of my favorite tools. But with AI generated em dashes every which way till Sunday, if you write a single dash, people will accuse you of AI slop. So, I'm a parentheses man now (see above) — except when an em dash really feels right.

I'm going now, but please subscribe to my newsletter if you're interested. Subscribers get exclusive worldbuilding documents you won't find here, and I promise I won't spam you. More is coming, including (I hope) some art soon. I'll share more about my art journey another time.

All the best,<br/>
Cody (S.C. Barrus)
